## Welcome to Centra One

Centra One brings inventory, purchasing, sales and your B2B portal into one place. This guide walks you through everything to do in your first 30 minutes, in order.

## Step 1: Complete your company setup (5 minutes)

Go to **Settings > Company**. Fill in your company name, address, logo and contact details. This information appears on all your invoices, quotes and purchase orders.

> **Tip**
>
> Upload your logo as a PNG or JPG for the best results. It appears on every customer-facing document.

## Step 2: Add your warehouses (2 minutes)

Go to **Inventory > Locations**. Add at least one warehouse: this is where your stock lives. Give it a clear name, like "Main Warehouse" or "Auckland DC".

## Step 3: Import your products (10 minutes)

Go to **Inventory > Products > Import**. Download the CSV template, fill in your product names, SKUs, costs and sell prices, then upload it. If you'd rather add products one at a time, click **New Product**.

> **Info**
>
> SKUs must be unique. If you're importing from another system, keep the same SKU format to avoid confusion.

## Step 4: Add your suppliers and customers (5 minutes)

Go to **Purchasing > Suppliers** to add your suppliers, and **Sales > Customers** to add your customers. You only need the basics to start: name, email and payment terms.

## Step 5: Set your opening stock (5 minutes)

Go to **Inventory > Stock Adjustments > New Adjustment**. Choose "Opening stock" as the reason and enter the current quantity for each product. This gives Centra One an accurate starting point.

## Step 6: You're ready

You can now create your first purchase order and sales order. The onboarding wizard in the app guides you through each step.
