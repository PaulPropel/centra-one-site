## What is a purchase order?

A purchase order (PO) is a formal document you send to a supplier to request goods. In Centra One, POs track what you've ordered, what has arrived and what you owe your suppliers.

## Creating a purchase order

1. Go to **Purchasing > Orders**.
2. Click **New Purchase Order**.
3. Select a supplier from the dropdown.
4. Set the order date and the expected delivery date.
5. Add product lines. Search by product name or SKU.
6. Enter the quantity and confirm the unit price.
7. Click **Save as Draft**.

## Sending the PO to your supplier

When you're happy with the PO, click **Send to Supplier**. Centra One emails a formatted PDF to your supplier's email address.

> **Important**
>
> Make sure your supplier's email address is correct in **Purchasing > Suppliers** before you send.

## Receiving goods against a PO

When your goods arrive:

1. Open the PO from **Purchasing > Orders**.
2. Click **Receive Goods**.
3. Enter the quantity received for each line.
4. Click **Confirm Receipt**.

Centra One updates your stock on hand automatically.

## Partially received orders

If only some of the goods have arrived, enter the quantities you received and save. The PO shows as Partially Received. When the rest arrives, repeat the process.
