## User roles in Centra One

Centra One has six user roles, each with a different level of access:

- **Admin:** full access to everything, including billing and user management.
- **Manager:** full access except billing settings.
- **Sales Rep:** access to customers, quotes and sales orders. Can't see cost prices or margins.
- **Warehouse Staff:** access to inventory, receiving and picking. Can't see financial data.
- **Accounts:** access to invoices, payments and purchasing, with a financial focus.
- **Read Only:** can view everything but can't make any changes.

## Inviting a new user

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Click **Invite User**.
3. Enter their email address and select their role.
4. Click **Send Invite**.

They'll receive an email with a link to set up their account.

## Changing a user's role

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Find the user and click their name.
3. Select the new role from the dropdown.
4. Click **Save**.

Role changes take effect immediately.

## Custom permissions

For finer control, admins can set custom permissions on individual users:

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Click the user.
3. Click **Custom permissions**.
4. Turn individual permissions on or off.
5. Click **Save**.

Custom permissions override the role's defaults for that user.

## Removing a user

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Click the user.
3. Click **Remove user**.
4. Confirm the removal.

The user loses access immediately. Their history (orders, notes and so on) is kept.
