## What is Centra AI?

Centra AI is the assistant built into Centra One. It can see your business data, including inventory, orders, customers, purchasing and financials. It answers questions, surfaces insights and flags what needs your attention. On the Growth and Scale plans it can also take action for you, with your approval.

## Asking Centra AI questions

Click the AI icon in the top navigation to open the chat panel. You can ask anything about your business:

- What is my best-selling product this month?
- Which customers have overdue invoices?
- How much stock do I have of Heavy Duty Degreaser 5L?
- Which products are expiring in the next 30 days?

Centra AI answers using your live business data, not generic advice.

## Centra AI actions

On the Growth and Scale plans, Centra AI can do more than answer questions. It can take action for you:

- Draft purchase orders for products below their reorder point
- Draft quotes
- Draft stock transfers between warehouses
- Draft quote follow-up emails
- Update product prices in bulk

Nothing is sent or saved until you approve it. Centra AI shows you exactly what it's about to do before you confirm, and every action is recorded in an audit trail in Settings.

> **Info**
>
> Asking questions and getting alerts are included on every plan. Actions are included on the Growth and Scale plans. See [pricing](/pricing).

## Growth Intel alerts

Centra AI monitors your business and raises an alert when something needs your attention:

- Low stock, before you run out
- Expiring batches, before the stock expires
- Overdue invoices
- Quotes that need a follow-up

Check the Growth Intel page to see all current alerts.

## Tips for getting the best from Centra AI

- **Be specific.** "What are my top 5 customers by revenue this month?" gets a better answer than "Who are my best customers?"
- **Ask for recommendations.** "What should I reorder this week?" gives you a list you can act on.
- **Use it for analysis.** "Compare my sales this month with last month" gives you the comparison in seconds.
