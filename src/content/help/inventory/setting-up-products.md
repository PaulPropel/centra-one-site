## Products in Centra One

Every item you buy and sell is a product in Centra One. Each product has a SKU (its unique identifier), a cost price, a sell price and a unit of measure.

## Creating a product by hand

1. Go to **Inventory > Products**.
2. Click **New Product**.
3. Fill in the required fields: Name, SKU and Sell price.
4. Add any optional fields: Cost price (needed for margin calculations), Description, Product type and Supplier.
5. Click **Save**.

## Importing products from a spreadsheet

For a large catalogue, use the import tool:

1. Go to **Inventory > Products > Import**.
2. Download the CSV template.
3. Fill in your products, one row per product.
4. Upload the completed file.
5. Review the import preview and confirm.

## Product tracking options

Centra One supports three types of inventory tracking:

- **Standard tracking:** tracks the total quantity on hand.
- **Serial number tracking:** each unit has its own serial number. Ideal for high-value equipment.
- **Batch/lot tracking:** units are grouped into batches with expiry dates. Ideal for food, chemicals and pharmaceuticals.

Turn tracking on from the product's **General** tab, under **Inventory Tracking**.

## Setting reorder points

To get reorder suggestions for a product:

1. Open the product.
2. Go to the **Inventory** tab.
3. Set the **Reorder Point** (the quantity at which to reorder) and the **Reorder Quantity** (how much to order).

Centra One suggests purchase orders automatically when stock falls below the reorder point.
